# Archived run summaries

Summaries here are copies of `results/<run>/summary.md` at the time of the run. Runs dated
before commit c39b208 (2026-10-15, "noise channel") coded the clean label law and are
superseded: a correct alignment paid tens of bits per flipped label, which is why trained
networks lost to their untrained twins there. Re-runs carry the suffix `_rr` and learner
names containing `rr=<rate>`.
