Reading. The regret of the valid alignment does not saturate: its local slope is 10-27 nats per ln n
up to n = 1024 and 41-47 over the last two doublings (d_V = 4), 23-78 throughout for d_V = 1. The MAP's
own mean training loss rises from 0.149 (1024 targets) to 0.170 (8192), i.e. the 1024-target fits were
partly interpolating (the frame has 260 x 4 = 1040 parameters against 1024 targets), and the earlier
"saturation" was measured inside that regime. Beyond n of about 2000 the data begin to constrain the
map and the regret grows at roughly 45 nats per ln n, which as an effective coefficient means about 90
constrained directions, 9% of the frame's parameters (nominal 512) and more than the 5-7 directions that
carry 90% of the empirical Fisher trace at n = 1024. Whether the slope settles there is open; runs to
n = 32768 (bitsev-ib-ha3x-dv4/dv1, EPYC) were submitted on 2026-10-20. The misspecified locus grows
much faster (91-115) from the excess-loss drift, as before; the ordering by faithfulness is unchanged.
