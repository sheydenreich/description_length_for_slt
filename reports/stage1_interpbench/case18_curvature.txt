Reading. On InterpBench the fitted point of the valid alignment is not a minimum in the sense the
curvature analysis needs: the loss is flat to 1e-4 and indefinite in 654 of 1024 directions, i.e. the
saturated-readout plateau found by the sampler traces (case18_llc.txt). Neither the Laplace count nor
the plug-in trace predicts the measured 8-45 per ln n, and that is the finding: on this testbed the
code's coefficient is not a local quantity at the fitted point but is paid at the boundary targets that
later blocks reveal. GPT-2 (curvature_gpt2.txt) is the opposite case, a regular point with a nearly flat
positive spectrum, where the plug-in trace predicts the coefficient within a factor 1.5. The two together
bound what a local, SLT-style analysis of an alignment map can deliver: it works where the readout is
unsaturated and the fit is a genuine optimum, and the prequential code remains the primary measurement.
