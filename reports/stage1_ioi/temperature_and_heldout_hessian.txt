Reading. The temperature removes the calibration advantage of the expressive maps: the valid subspace's
clean-target loss drops to the channel floor, and the fit term against the manufactured alignment is −4 bits
of 9265. With every predictor calibrated, the verdict between the valid and the manufactured explanation is
carried entirely by the regret term, i.e. by the learner coefficients; no crossover by 32768 targets. The
trained coupling stays ~1100 bits behind (fit term −99 ± ~400, within noise). Against label-only and the twin
the gain is all fit term with or without the temperature.

Untempered, the gain against the forgery peaks at 16384 and shrinks; the coupling wins by 6.1k bits. Tempered,
the gain peaks at 32768 and dips by 1.2k over the last doubling. The fit/regret split is NOT reliable at
131072: the learner's budget is fixed (400 Adam steps, minibatches of 256) whatever n, so the final fit on
131072 targets is under-optimised (tempered manufactured final fit 0.064 nats above the floor in-sample
against 0.012 for its own block predictor on the last block). Decide the large-n behaviour on the three
seeds and with a converged final fit before quoting it.
