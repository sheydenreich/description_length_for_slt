Reading. (1) On GPT-2 the valid alignment's coefficient is stable at roughly 45-50 per ln n from n = 32
to n = 16384, i.e. about 100 constrained directions of the 12288-parameter frame, with no sign of the
interpolation plateau seen on InterpBench below its parameter count; the negative last step (-36) is
the final fit on 32768 targets being slightly worse than the block predictor (400 cold-start Adam steps on
minibatches of 256 of the 32768 pairs; corrected 2026-10-22, the run used no L-BFGS), a limitation of the best-in-family proxy, not a feature of the code. (2) The
manufactured alignment pays 6775 nats of regret by 32768 targets, 18x the valid alignment's 365; its
local coefficient peaks at 1700-2450 between n = 512 and 2048, where new base/source combinations
expose what the expressive map memorised, and settles near 600-1300 afterwards. (3) The expressive
family on the trained network pays 165-195 throughout: the coupling map's coefficient is set by the
family, the manufactured alignment's 4x larger one by the untrained network it is attached to. (4) The
untrained twin's own subspace code grows faster at large n (41 -> 66): with more pairs the twin's
copy of the name tokens is read out progressively. Figure: reports/talk_slt/gpt2_regret_vs_n.png.
