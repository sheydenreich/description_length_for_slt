Stage 1 on IOI in larger language models (2026-10-21). Same protocol as GPT-2 (1024 targets, noise 0.05,
geometric schedule r = 2, d_V 16, subspace map, 400 map steps; learner: the single-map MAP code (laplace, no
samples) for Llama and Qwen2.5-3B, the mixture-of-4 for Qwen3 (settings.json of each run); manufactured
alignment = coupling map with 4000 steps on the untrained twin at the strongest valid locus). Label-only
code 4324.5 bits for every model (same targets, hash 927e660f3575b1b1). Loci chosen from the map sweeps
(map_sweep_<model>.json). Summaries: llama31_8b_summary.md, qwen25_3b_summary.md; Qwen3 from
results/stage1_ioi_qwen3_17b/records (3 seeds; the manufactured-alignment arm finished after the first archive
and was added on 2026-10-22).

Gains over label-only in bits (IIA on the i.i.d. split); "twin" = same subspace family on the untrained
twin, "coupling" = coupling family on the trained network, "wrong locus" = layer 0.

Reading. (1) The GPT-2 verdicts transfer to models 20-50x larger without any tuning: at the loci where
the name is intervenable the valid subspace gains 3000-3100 bits on every model (Llama 3101, Qwen2.5-3B
3129, Qwen3 3042, GPT-2 3385 with the same single-map MAP code), the controls are rejected at alpha 0.01 within the first 10-20 targets,
and the manufactured alignment never beats label-only (-1700 to -1800 bits under the single-map code; -147
under Qwen3's mixture of 4, which rescues confident errors as it did for GPT-2's +704) although its IIA
(0.51-0.71) would pass an accuracy threshold of 0.5. All at 1024 targets: on GPT-2 at 32768 targets the
manufactured alignment beats label-only by 108956 bits (gain_decomposition_gpt2_n32768.txt). (2) The coupling family on the trained network is always
1000-1700 bits behind the subspace at the same locus with the same or higher IIA: the code charges the
family's complexity, which IIA does not see. (3) The gain is nearly model-independent because it is
dominated by n times the loss gain of a near-perfect predictor on 1016 targets behind the 0.05 noise
channel; the differences between models are in the controls (twin strength 0.27-0.57) and in where the
intervenable loci sit (last 2-4 layers in every model; Qwen2.5-3B needs layer 34 of 36, Llama 28-30
of 32).
