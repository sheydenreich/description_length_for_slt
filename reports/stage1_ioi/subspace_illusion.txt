Subspace-illusion check (Makelov et al. 2024) for the learned IOI subspaces (2026-10-22; review item 2.3).
Code: src/bitsev/experiments/ioi_illusion.py. Results: results/ioi_illusion_gpt2, _pythia14, _qwen05 (illusion.json).
Jobs 1827642 (GPT-2, 6 min), 1827643 (Pythia-1.4b, 58 min), 1827644 (Qwen2.5-0.5B, 8 min), one A100 each.

Protocol. Same IOI pool and 1024 training targets as the Stage 1 runs (variable io, final-position residual,
d_V = 16). Per locus and seed (0, 1, 2), one cold-start single-map fit of the subspace map (Adam, 400 steps,
lr 1e-2, minibatch 256, observed-law loss): the plug-in learner. The output-connected subspace C_tau of the locus
is spanned by the top right singular vectors of the stacked per-prompt Jacobians d log softmax(32 names) / d h
(256 training bases), holding a share tau of their energy; k_tau is its dimension. The fitted U is split into
P_C U (connected part) and (I - P_C) U (disconnected part); each part's span is patched alone and scored by IIA
on the 1000-pair i.i.d. split. The level of a patch that changes nothing is 0.072 (share of pairs whose clean
counterfactual equals the base's own answer). Directions of U are ordered by readout sensitivity; for the most
sensitive one: sensitivity and variance across training bases relative to the median of 256 random directions,
and the share of its variance explained by the IO name (random directions in brackets). Mean +- s.e. over seeds.

Reading. (1) No illusion at any locus of the three models. The illusion needs a disconnected part that carries
the variable and a dormant connected part that the patch activates, so that neither part alone works. Here the
disconnected part alone is at the no-effect level (0.072-0.073) everywhere, and the connected part alone (99%
of the Jacobian energy) reproduces the full IIA (0.93-1.00) except at Pythia layer 16 (0.80 against 0.93, 0.89 at
99.9%), where part of the effect runs through directions of low first-order sensitivity. (2) The sensitive
directions are not dormant: the most sensitive direction of U varies 6-43x more across prompts than a random
direction, and the IO name explains 82-91% of its variance against 23-54% for random directions. Sensitivity,
variance and the variable sit in the same directions. (3) 21-52% of each U lies outside C_99 and does nothing
when patched. At the final loci (GPT-2 layer 12, Qwen layer 24), where C is the 31-dimensional row space of the
name unembedding, about half of U is such dead weight, and patching the connected part alone slightly raises IIA
(0.99 against 0.97-0.98). The learner has no gradient that removes those directions; they do not affect the code.
(4) Caveat: at a residual-stream locus every later layer reads the state, so the split is by local sensitivity
at the base states, not by the row space of one linear map as in Makelov et al.; the patches themselves are
nonlinear and are what (1) is based on. The check covers the learned subspace on IOI only; the coupling maps and
the other testbeds are not checked.
