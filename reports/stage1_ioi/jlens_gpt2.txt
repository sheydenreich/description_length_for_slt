=============================================================================================
Run: results/ioi_jlens_gpt2 (job 1813832, 2026-10-20; jlens_gpt2.json, jlens_gpt2_jacobian_consistency.json).
J-vectors: gradient of each name's final logit w.r.t. the residual at the locus (final position),
averaged over 1000 prompts; frame = span of the unit J-vectors ("all": 32 names of the output
alphabet, rank 32; "train": the 16 training names, rank 16). The alignment is the subspace

predictor's loss on every target and the learning coefficient is 0. Same 1024 targets and the
same label-only code (4324.5 bits) as every GPT-2 Stage 1 run.

Reading. (1) H-A10 holds on every count. At the loci where the name is verbalizable (layers 11, 12)
the parameter-free alignment is as faithful as the learned subspace (0.98-1.00) and its code is
*shorter*: +3529 / +3626 bits against +3302 / +3145 for the learned subspace under the mixture code
and +3385 for the single MAP map at layer 11. At layer 10 the learned map wins (+2975 vs +2821; IIA
0.97 vs 0.80), at layer 9 and below the lens fails (0.07, the chance level for 16 names) while the
learned subspace also fails there. (2) On the untrained twin it fails everywhere (IIA 0.04, code 850
bits longer than label-only), as predicted for a copied variable the twin cannot route. (3) The
Jacobian-consistency statistic separates the manufactured alignment from both valid ones by a factor
6-10 at the median (2.0-2.2 against 0.15-0.38): the manufactured map moves the output through local
sensitivity that the network's averaged Jacobian does not show. (4) The "all" variant generalises to
the 16 unseen names (0.99) because their directions are in the frame; the "train" variant, the fair
comparison, matches the learned map's failure on unseen names (0.07): generalisation across names is
a property of which directions are in the frame, not of learning. (5) For the complexity ladder: the
lens sits at learning coefficient 0 with the shortest code of all explanations of this variable; the
learned subspace pays its coefficient (49 under the MAP code at n <= 1024) without buying a shorter
code; the coupling and the manufactured alignment pay 226 and 988. Distortion of the bijection
phi_J (cond(V_J)): 68-121 at layers 9-11 on the trained network, 1.3-1.5 on the twin, 4.6-7.6 at
the final locus; the interchange itself is an isometry. Figure: reports/talk_slt/gpt2_complexity_ladder.png.
