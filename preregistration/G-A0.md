## Criteria

1. **Batching factor reproduced.** For the Gaussian mean, fitted logarithmic coefficients
   of the expected cumulative excess at block ends in [2⁸, 2²²] lie within 2·10⁻³ of
   ½(r−1)/ln r for r ∈ {1, 1.5, 2, 4} (plug-in) and r ∈ {1, 2} (Bayes). *Status: met in
   week 1; largest deviation 6·10⁻⁴.*
2. **Bayes redundancy isolated.** For each quadrature model the per-sample Bayes code
   F_n − n L_n(w₀), fitted against ln n over at least one decade with n ≤ 10⁴, has slope
   within 0.05 of λ for the m = 1 models. For the m = 2 models the slope is reported with
   the (log log n) term fixed to its theoretical value and without it; the plan does not
   claim to estimate m.
3. **Batching isolated.** For the same models, block codes with r = 2 and r = 4 have
   uncorrected slopes within 0.05·(r−1)/ln r of λ(r−1)/ln r, and corrected slopes within
   0.05 of λ. The correction is applied in analysis only; stored losses are raw.
4. **Optimizer effect isolated.** For reduced-rank regression and for at least one
   quadrature model, a plug-in (maximum-likelihood or gradient-trained) learner has a
   fitted coefficient κ that differs from λ by more than three standard errors of the fit,
   while the Bayes code of the same family satisfies criterion 2. If κ = λ within error in
   every model tried, the gate still passes but the result is recorded as "no optimizer
   effect observed at this scale" and T4 stays open.
5. **Thermometer and identifiability** (carried-over checks) reproduce the plan's
   Appendix C numbers: thermometer T̂/T and λ̂ for x²y² within 0.01 of (0.93, 0.97, 0.98)
   and (0.37, 0.40, 0.42); identifiability shows VIF > 100 over one to three decades.

## Decision

G-A0 passes if criteria 1, 2, 3 and 5 are met and criterion 4 has been run. A failure of
2 or 3 means the estimators, not the theory, are suspect: the plan's week 3 work does not
start until the cause is found, up to a budget of three working days, after which the gate
is reported as failed.

## Fixed settings

- Quadrature: tensor-product Gauss–Legendre on [−1, 1]² (box prior), node count chosen by
  doubling until F_n changes by less than 10⁻⁸ at the largest n; recorded per model.
- Sample sizes for slope fits: n ∈ {2⁴, …, 2¹⁴} for quadrature models.
- Random seed for any sampling: 20261014. Sampler settings recorded in the result files.
- Reporting: `results/G-A0/report.md`, generated by `bitsev-exact` from result files.
